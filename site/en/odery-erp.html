Deliverable Custom ERP/CRM

Odery Drums runs sales, catalog, international importing and after-sales at the same time, at the volume of one of the world's largest drum manufacturers. Each area has its own logic: the sales funnel needs to know what's in the catalog, the catalog comes from Sankhya, importing carries its own exchange rates, ICMS and customs-clearance timelines. Odery ERP brings the six areas into a single system, with AI in the service layer.

The Scenario

Six areas.
One
system.

We could have wired Sankhya to an off-the-shelf CRM. That would have been faster. Instead, Odery ERP was built around the real operation: sales, catalog, importing, after-sales, team and marketing sharing the same database, with role-based permissions and history that doesn't get lost between departments. Each area gets the interface the people in that area actually work with day to day, not a generic management-system screen.

Results

One operation,
in one
place.

The system brings sales, catalog, importing, after-sales, team and marketing together on one database. The catalog never needs re-entering, because it comes straight from Sankhya. Importing keeps every process tracked from order to stock. And AI sits next to each step: it captures what was said on a call, turns it into a task, and answers when someone on the team asks what was already agreed with a client.
