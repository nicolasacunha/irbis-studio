WhatsApp · Odery Drums
The Scenario

WhatsApp became the front door. No one was guarding it.

An instrument buyer searches, gets interested, and messages on WhatsApp. But the volume is high, the questions repeat, and many people write outside business hours. When every reply is manual, the lead waits, cools off, or disappears, and no one logs that the conversation ever happened. Tom was designed to hold that entry point: it receives the message, replies on the spot, formalizes consent, and stores the contact, so the sales team picks up the conversation with data in hand, not from scratch.

What we solved

The first contact, on autopilot.

01

Opening and identity. Tom introduces itself as Odery's assistant and carries the conversation in the brand's language, without sounding like a stuck robot.

02

LGPD consent on record. Before collecting any data, consent to the Privacy Policy is presented and recorded. Compliance enters at the start of the flow.

03

Lead registration. Name and contact details are collected in the conversation and stored, so sales can continue from where Tom left off.

04

Catalog on the spot. The menu delivers the right material in the same conversation: video, PDF catalog, and the choice by brand, without handing off to a human.

05

Available 24/7. The entry point stays open outside business hours, when much of the audience writes.

Conversation 02 · Menu and catalog

The customer chooses. Tom delivers.

Through the guided menu the customer asks for the catalog and gets the PDF on the spot, then is guided to pick the brand. All within WhatsApp, without leaving the conversation and without waiting for an agent.

What it delivers
